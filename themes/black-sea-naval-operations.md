# Current Situation

Ukrainian Magura V5 naval drones armed with missiles destroyed two Russian Mi-8 helicopters and damaged a third in the Black Sea on December 31, 2024. The loss of highly trained helicopter crews is significant.

Ukrainian strikes against occupied Crimea have deprived Russian forces of sustainable logistics in the area, including damage to the railway on the Kerch Strait Bridge and ferries that transported railway cars and fuel tankers. Russian military vessels are reportedly trying to hide from Ukrainian naval strikes in bays and ports.

ISW assesses that increased Ukrainian offensive capabilities in the Black Sea threaten Russian control over occupied Crimea.[1]

The GUR reported on January 3 that several Russian ships will arrive at the Port of Tartus in Syria to evacuate Russian military assets to Libya. The Ivan Gren large landing ship, Alexander Otrakovsky landing ship, and Sparta cargo ship are scheduled to arrive on January 5; the Sparta II cargo ship and Ivan Skobelev tanker on January 8. The vessels will transfer Russian assets to an unspecified location in Libya.[2]
