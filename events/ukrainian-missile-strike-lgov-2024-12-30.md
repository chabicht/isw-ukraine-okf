Ukrainian forces launched Storm Shadow missiles at the command post of the Russian 76th Airborne (VDV) Division near Lgov, Kursk Oblast on December 30, 2024. The strike killed the division's Communications Head Lieutenant Colonel Valery Tereschchenko. A Ukrainian source reported that the strike killed eight senior commanders and wounded 20 personnel. The strike targeted Russian command and control in Kursk Oblast in support of ongoing Ukrainian operations in the salient.[1]
