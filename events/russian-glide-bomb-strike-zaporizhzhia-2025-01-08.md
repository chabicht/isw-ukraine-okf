# Event

On January 8, 2025, Russian forces conducted a glide bomb strike against an industrial infrastructure object in Zaporizhzhia City, killing 13 people and injuring at least 116 — an increase from the 63 initially reported on January 8. Zaporizhia Oblast Military Administration Head Ivan Fedorov reported the updated casualty figure on January 9.[1][2]
