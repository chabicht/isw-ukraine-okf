# Event

On January 8, 2025, Russian forces conducted a glide bomb strike against an industrial infrastructure object in Zaporizhzhia City, killing 13 people and injuring at least 63. Zaporizhia Oblast Military Administration Head Ivan Fedorov reported the strike.[1]
