On January 1, 2025, Russian gas ceased flowing through Ukrainian territory after Ukraine declined to renew the transit contract. The cessation costs Gazprom an estimated $6 billion annually. The only remaining Russian gas route to Europe is the TurkStream pipeline. Slovakia and Austria have met their energy needs through alternative sources. Russia's share of the European gas market dropped from 45 percent in 2021 to approximately 5 percent.[1]
