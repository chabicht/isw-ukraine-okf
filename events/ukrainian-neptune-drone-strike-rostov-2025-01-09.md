On the night of January 9 to 10, Ukrainian forces struck a Russian ammunition and drone storage warehouse near Chaltyr, Rostov Oblast with drones and Neptune anti-ship cruise missiles. Sources within Ukraine's Security Service (SBU) told Ukrainian outlets that Ukrainian forces used the drones to overwhelm and exhaust Russian air defenses in the area before launching Neptune missiles at the warehouse. The sources stated that Russian forces use reconnaissance drones from this warehouse to correct Russian strikes on Ukrainian cities and frontline positions. Rostov Oblast Governor Yury Slyusar stated that Russian forces downed 16 Ukrainian drones over the oblast and that the strike caused a fire at an industrial enterprise just north of Chaltyr.[1]
