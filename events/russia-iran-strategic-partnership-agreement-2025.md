Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian is expected to sign a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Agreement with Russia in Moscow on January 17, 2025. The agreement likely includes provisions for Russia to supply Iran with Su-35 fighter jets, technology transfers for missile and military satellite production, and S-400 advanced defense systems. Iran may send shipments of upgraded strike drone models to Russia in return. Sources speculate Iran may also transfer Fath-360 short-range ballistic missiles and air defense missile systems to Russia.[1]
