On December 31, 2024, a Ukrainian Magura V5 naval drone used missiles to destroy two Russian Mi-8 helicopters and damage a third in the Black Sea. Ukraine's Main Military Intelligence Directorate specified these details on January 2, upgrading the initial report of one helicopter destroyed. The loss of highly trained helicopter crews represents a significant operational setback for Russian forces in the Black Sea theater.[1]
