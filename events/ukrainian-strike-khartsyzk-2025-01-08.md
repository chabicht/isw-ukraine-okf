# Event

On January 8, 2025, Ukrainian forces struck a command post of the Russian 8th Combined Arms Army (Southern Military District) in occupied Khartsyzk, Donetsk Oblast. The Ukrainian General Staff reported that the 8th CAA used this command post to coordinate operations in the Kurakhove direction.[1]
