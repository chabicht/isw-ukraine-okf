# Event

On January 12, 2025, Ukrainian forces conducted a high-precision airstrike on the command post of Russia's 2nd Combined Arms Army (CAA, Central Military District) in Novohrodivka, Donetsk Oblast. The Ukrainian General Staff reported that the operation is part of a broader series of Ukrainian strikes targeting command posts of Russian forces operating in the Donetsk direction.[1]

Ukrainian forces had previously struck the command posts of the Russian 8th CAA (Southern Military District) in occupied Khartsyzk on January 8 and the 3rd Army Corps (Central Military District) in occupied Svitlodarsk on January 10. The strike on the 2nd CAA command post near the frontline in Novohrodivka was likely intended to disrupt Russian tactical activity and complicate Russian command and control on the battlefield. By contrast, strikes against main command posts further in the Russian rear, such as the January 8 strike on the 8th CAA post, are likely aimed at degrading broader Russian logistics and operational planning.[1]

ISW has observed that the 2nd CAA is currently leading Russian operations south of Pokrovsk, that the 3rd AC is operating near Chasiv Yar, and that the 8th CAA is leading Russian efforts near Kurakhove.[1]
