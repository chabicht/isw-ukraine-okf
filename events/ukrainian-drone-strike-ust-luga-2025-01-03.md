# Background

Ust-Luga is the second largest sea trade port in Russia after Novorossiysk, Krasnodar Krai. The Novatek gas terminal at the port had been struck by Ukrainian forces in January 2024 as well.[1]

# Event

On the night of January 3 to 4, 2025, Ukrainian forces struck the Novatek gas terminal at the Ust-Luga port in Leningrad Oblast. Geolocated footage published on January 4 shows damage to the terminal. Russian opposition outlet _Astra_ reported that Russian forces shot down most of the drones over the port terminal of Novatek subsidiary Novatrans LLC, causing minor damage to a building. Leningrad Oblast Governor Alexander Drozdenko claimed that Russian forces downed a total of four drones over Leningrad Oblast and that there was no damage, though geolocated evidence contradicts this.[1]
