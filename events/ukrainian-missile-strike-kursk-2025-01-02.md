Ukrainian forces conducted a missile strike, possibly with HIMARS, against the command post of the Russian 810th Naval Infantry Brigade in Ivanovskoye, Kursk Oblast on January 2. Geolocated footage showed a damaged building and Russian military vehicles at the site. Reports indicate the 810th Brigade was holding an award ceremony at the House of Culture at the time of the strike.[1]
