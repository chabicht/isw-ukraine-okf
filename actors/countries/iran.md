# Current Situation

Iran and Russia are expected to sign a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Agreement in Moscow on January 17, 2025, as confirmed by the Iranian Embassy and Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov.[1]

Per Israeli media reports, the agreement likely includes provisions for Russia to supply Iran with Su-35 fighter jets, technology transfers for missile and military satellite production, and advanced air defense systems such as the S-400. Iran is expected to send upgraded strike drone models to Russia in return.[1]

Russian milbloggers have speculated that Iran is preparing to transfer Fath-360 short-range ballistic missiles and Arman or Ababil air defense missile systems to Russia from the Bandar Anzali port on the Caspian Sea, though some analysts questioned whether the imagery supports these claims.[1]

Iran has already supplied Russia with Shahed drones throughout the war. Ukrainian President Zelensky reported that Ukrainian forces shot down 7,800 Iranian Shahed drones in 2024 alone.[1]
