# Current Situation

President Zelensky used his New Year's Eve address on December 31, 2024 to signal that Ukraine will increase drone and missile strikes against Russia in 2025 as part of a strategy to compel Russia to accept a "just peace" in future negotiations.[1]

Zelensky announced that Ukraine produced over one million drones in 2024 and is actively building domestic missile systems including Palyanytsya, Peklo, Ruta, Neptune, and Sapsan — some of which have yet to be used operationally. He emphasized that Ukraine's defense industrial base produced 30 percent of all military equipment used on the frontlines in 2024.[1]

Zelensky characterized Ukraine's expanding missile and drone arsenal as "arguments for a just peace," articulating a strategic vision in which military strength creates the conditions for favorable diplomacy. He also reported that Ukrainian forces shot down 1,310 cruise and ballistic missiles and 7,800 Iranian Shahed drones in 2024.[1]
