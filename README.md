# ISW Russia-Ukraine Knowledge Bundle

A structured knowledge base on the Russia-Ukraine war — actors, conflicts,
regions, themes, and events — synthesized from the Institute for the Study of
War's near-daily *Russian Offensive Campaign Assessment* series and ISW
special reports.

## Directories

* [Actors](actors/) — Countries, leaders, organizations, and analysts
* [Conflicts](conflicts/) — Wars, crises, and sustained standoffs
* [Regions](regions/) — Geographic areas with independent dynamics, including the recurring ISW frontline axes
* [Themes](themes/) — Cross-cutting analytical topics and frameworks
* [Events](events/) — Discrete occurrences
