# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations along the Kupyansk-Svatove-Kreminna line on January 3 but made no confirmed advances. A Russian milblogger continued to claim Russian forces crossed the Zherebets River and advanced to Ivanivka (northwest of Kreminna), but ISW has still not observed confirmation of this claim. Ground attacks continued north of Kupyansk near Zapadne and Dvorichna; southeast of Kupyansk near Zahryzove and Lozova; west of Svatove near Nadiya and Kruhlyakivka; northwest of Kreminna near Makiivka, Terny, and Cherneshchyna; and southwest of Kreminna near Hryhorivka and the Serebryanske forest area.[1]
