# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations in the Kupyansk direction on January 5 but did not make any confirmed advances. A Russian milblogger claimed that Russian forces advanced near Zapadne (north of Kupyansk), but ISW has not observed confirmation. Russian forces attacked northeast of Kupyansk near Petropavlivka. Ukrainian Kupyansk City Administration Head Andriy Besedin stated that the frontline is approximately two kilometers from the city's center and that Russian forces are conducting glide bomb, MLRS, and drone strikes on Kupyansk.[1]

Russian forces recently advanced in the Borova direction amid continued offensive operations. Geolocated footage indicates that Russian forces recently advanced in fields northwest of Lozova (northeast of Borova) during a roughly company-sized mechanized assault. The Ukrainian Khortytsia Group of Forces reported that Russian forces conducted a mechanized assault near Zahryzove (north of Borova) and that Ukrainian forces damaged two armored vehicles while repelling the assault. Russian forces attacked north of Borova near Zahryzove; east of Borova near Nadiya, Pershotravneve, Novoserhiivka, Novoyehorivka, and Tverdokhlibove; and southeast of Borova near Makiivka.[1]

Ukrainian and Russian forces recently advanced northwest of Kreminna in the Lyman direction. Geolocated footage published on January 2 shows that Ukrainian forces recaptured positions in central Terny. Additional geolocated footage published on January 4 shows that Russian forces advanced across the Zherebets River and into central Ivanivka. Russian forces also continued ground attacks northwest of Kreminna near Yampolivka and Terny and southwest of Kreminna in the Serebryanske forest area.[1]
