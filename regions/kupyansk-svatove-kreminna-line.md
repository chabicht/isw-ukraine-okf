# Current Situation

Russian forces reportedly crossed the Zherebets River and advanced west of Ivanivka (northwest of Kreminna). Russian sources also claimed advances in southern Terny (northwest of Kreminna), though Russian forces have not yet completely seized Terny. ISW has not confirmed these claims. Elements of the Russian 27th Motorized Rifle Brigade (1st Guards Tank Army), 15th Motorized Rifle Regiment (2nd Motorized Rifle Division), and 272nd Motorized Rifle Regiment (47th Tank Division) are operating in the direction. Russian forces are using fiber-optic cable drones, which Ukrainian forces counter by cutting the cables or shooting the drones down with small arms. [1]
