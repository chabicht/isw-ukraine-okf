# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations in the Kupyansk direction on January 6 but did not advance. Russian forces attacked northeast of Kupyansk near Petropavlivka and Synkivka. The commander of a Ukrainian drone battalion operating in the Kupyansk direction stated that Russian forces are using armored vehicle columns to transport infantry to the frontline, with mine-clearing vehicles leading, tanks providing fire support, and infantry fighting vehicles carrying five to seven personnel each. Russian forces use more infantry in assaults when unable to use armored vehicles, with dismounted infantry sometimes walking up to 10 kilometers to reach the frontline. Poor weather sometimes prevents Russian forces from fielding armored vehicles, aircraft, or drones, and Russian forces send infantry without reconnaissance support in these conditions. Russian forces conducted strikes targeting crossings over the Oskil River in the Kupyansk direction on the night of January 5 to 6.[1]

Russian forces continued offensive operations in the Borova direction on January 6 but did not advance. A Russian milblogger claimed that Russian forces advanced in fields east of Zahryzove and west of Lozova (both northeast of Borova) on January 5, but ISW has not observed confirmation. Geolocated footage shows Russian forces conducted a roughly platoon-sized mechanized assault east of Kruhlyakivka (northeast of Borova) without making confirmed advances. Russian forces attacked northeast of Borova near Nova Kruhlyakivka and Zahryzove; east of Borova near Nadiya and Pershotravneve; and southeast of Borova near Novoserhiivka, Tverdokhlibove, and Makiivka.[1]

Russian forces recently advanced northwest of Kreminna in the Lyman direction. Geolocated footage indicates that Russian forces — reportedly elements of the Russian 144th Motorized Rifle Division (20th Combined Arms Army, Moscow Military District) — likely seized Ivanivka and advanced southwest of the settlement as well as in fields northwest of Terny (both northwest of Kreminna). A Russian milblogger claimed further advances in fields southwest of Ivanivka, though ISW has not observed visual confirmation of the extent. Russian forces continued ground attacks northwest of Kreminna near Terny and west of Kreminna near Zarichne.[1]
