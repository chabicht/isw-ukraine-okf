# Current Situation

Russian forces continued limited ground attacks northeast of Kharkiv City near Vovchansk on January 5 and 6 but did not make any confirmed advances. Kharkiv Oblast Military Administration Head Oleh Synehubov stated on January 6 that Ukraine's renewed offensive operations in Kursk Oblast have already reduced the number of Russian ground attacks in northern Kharkiv Oblast.[1]
