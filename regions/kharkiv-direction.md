# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations near Vovchansk (northeast of Kharkiv City) on January 2 and 3 but made no confirmed advances. A Russian milblogger claimed Ukrainian forces counterattacked four times near Lyptsi (north of Kharkiv City) on January 3. The Pivnich Operational Command reported that Russian sabotage and reconnaissance groups are mostly operating during poor weather in the border areas with drone and artillery support, and that these groups have significantly reduced levels of training.[1]
