# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations near Vovchansk (northeast of Kharkiv City) from December 31 through January 2 but made no confirmed advances. Ukrainian forces are repelling Russian sabotage and reconnaissance groups attempting to infiltrate Ukrainian positions in Vovchansk. [1]
