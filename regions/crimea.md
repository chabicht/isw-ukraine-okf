# Current Situation

Ukrainian strikes continue to degrade Russian logistics in occupied Crimea, including damage to the railway on the Kerch Strait Bridge and ferries that transported railway cars and fuel tankers. Russian military vessels are trying to hide from Ukrainian naval drone strikes in bays and ports. The GUR reported new details on the December 31 naval drone strike that destroyed two Russian Mi-8 helicopters and damaged a third using missiles from a Magura V5 naval drone. ISW assesses that increased Ukrainian offensive capabilities in the Black Sea will likely threaten Russian control over occupied Crimea. [1]
