# Current Situation

Elements of the Russian 7th Motorized Rifle Brigade (3rd Combined Arms Army) recently advanced in central Bilohorivka (northeast of Siversk), confirmed by geolocated footage published on January 2.[1] Russian forces continued ground attacks near Bilohorivka on January 2 and 3 but made no additional confirmed advances.[2]
