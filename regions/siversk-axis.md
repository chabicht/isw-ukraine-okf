# Current Situation

Elements of the Russian 7th Motorized Rifle Brigade (3rd Combined Arms Army) recently advanced in central Bilohorivka (northeast of Siversk), confirmed by geolocated footage published on January 2. Russian forces continued ground attacks near Bilohorivka and Serebryanka from December 31 to January 2. [1]
