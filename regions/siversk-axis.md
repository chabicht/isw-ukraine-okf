# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations near Bilohorivka (northeast of Siversk) on January 5 and 6 but did not make any confirmed advances. Ukrainian Luhansk Oblast Military Administration Head Artem Lysohor published footage on January 6 showing Russian forces unsuccessfully attacking in the Siversk direction with fireteams consisting of two to four personnel.[1]
