# Current Situation

Russian forces continued attacks on islands in the Dnipro River Delta from December 31 to January 2. Drone operators of the Russian 205th Motorized Rifle Brigade (49th CAA, Southern Military District) are operating in the Kherson direction.[1]

Russian forces continued attacks near Kozatskyi Island (east of Kherson City) on January 2 and 3. Drone operators of the Russian 80th Arctic Motorized Rifle Brigade (14th AC, Leningrad Military District) are operating in the Kherson direction.[2]
