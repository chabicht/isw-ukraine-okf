# Current Situation

Russian forces continued attacks on islands in the Dnipro River Delta from December 31 to January 2. Drone operators of the Russian 205th Motorized Rifle Brigade (49th CAA, Southern Military District) are operating in the Kherson direction.[1]

Russian forces continued attacks near Kozatskyi Island (east of Kherson City) on January 2 and 3.[2]

Russian forces continued assaults in the Dnipro direction and on the islands in the Dnipro River Delta on January 3 and 4 but did not make any confirmed advances.[3]

Russian forces continued limited ground attacks in the Dnipro direction on January 5 and 6 but did not make any confirmed advances. Ukraine's Southern Operational Command reported on January 6 that Russian forces are attempting to seize a bridgehead in the island zone in the Dnipro River Delta. Elements of the Russian 80th Arctic Motorized Brigade (14th Army Corps, Leningrad Military District) reportedly continue to operate in the Kherson direction.[4]
