# Current Situation

Russian forces recently advanced in northwestern and western Toretsk following a reduced-platoon sized mechanized assault, confirmed by geolocated footage published on January 1. ISW assesses Russian forces occupy roughly 65% of Toretsk. Drone operators of the Russian 9th Motorized Rifle Brigade (51st CAA) conducted strikes enabling elements of the 1st Motorized Rifle Brigade to advance. The 132nd Motorized Rifle Brigade (51st CAA) is also operating in the direction. Russian forces are using fog and smoke grenades to conceal ground attacks. [1]
