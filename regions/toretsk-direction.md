# Current Situation

Russian forces recently advanced in northwestern Toretsk following several weeks of higher tempo Russian offensive operations. Geolocated footage published on January 6 indicates Russian forces recently advanced along Kvitkova Street and reached the northwestern administrative boundary of Toretsk. A Russian milblogger claimed additional gains along Pyrohova Street in northern Toretsk, but ISW has not observed confirmation. ISW assesses Russian forces occupy approximately 71 percent of Toretsk as of January 7. Russian forces intensified offensive operations in the Toretsk direction in June 2024, originally committing limited combat power including elements of the 51st Combined Arms Army, Territorial Troops, and some elements of the Central Military District.[1]

Russian forces are shifting assault tactics in Toretsk, attacking in platoons of up to 20 soldiers after previously using fireteams of roughly five personnel. Russian forces are likely leveraging superior manpower to intensify operations and advance within Toretsk. A Russian milblogger claimed recent gains resulted from attacking in multiple areas at once instead of focusing on one location. The 132nd and 9th motorized rifle brigades and 80th "Sparta" Separate Reconnaissance Battalion (51st CAA) have been the main forces conducting offensive operations, with unconfirmed reports of elements of the 55th Motorized Rifle Brigade (41st CAA, CMD) also operating in the area.[1]

Russian forces likely intend to exploit advances in northwestern Toretsk to push further west along the T-05-16 Toretsk-Kostyantynivka highway towards the southern point of Ukraine's fortress belt in Kostyantynivka. Russian positions are currently 12 kilometers from the southeastern outskirts of Kostyantynivka. Russian forces could threaten Kostyantynivka within two or three months but only if they achieve a rate of advance in open areas northwest of Toretsk similar to the Kurakhove and Pokrovsk directions, which would require significant reinforcement. The current Toretsk force grouping is smaller than those in Pokrovsk, Kurakhove, and Vuhledar and likely does not have prioritized access to Russian reserves.[1]

Russian forces may also attempt to leverage gains within and near Toretsk with recent advances east of Pokrovsk to eliminate the Ukrainian salient southwest of Toretsk. Advances northwest of Toretsk and south of Chasiv Yar could collapse the Ukrainian pocket in the area and level the frontline west and southwest of Kostyantynivka. The Russian military command could redeploy forces from the Pokrovsk and Kurakhove directions to Toretsk in the future but would likely not do so until objectives in those areas are achieved. A Russian milblogger claimed the command withdrew elements of the 5th and 110th motorized rifle brigades (51st CAA) from Kurakhove for rest and reconstitution, potentially for future commitment to a priority sector that could include Toretsk.[1]
