# Current Situation

Russian forces recently advanced in northwestern and western Toretsk following a reduced-platoon sized mechanized assault, confirmed by geolocated footage published on January 1. ISW assesses Russian forces occupy roughly 65% of Toretsk. Drone operators of the Russian 9th Motorized Rifle Brigade (51st CAA) conducted strikes enabling elements of the 1st Motorized Rifle Brigade to advance. The 132nd Motorized Rifle Brigade (51st CAA) is also operating in the direction. Russian forces are using fog and smoke grenades to conceal ground attacks.[1]

Russian forces continued offensive operations in the Toretsk direction on January 3 but made no confirmed advances. A Ukrainian mortar battery commander reported that Russian forces are disguising themselves in civilian clothing to bypass Ukrainian frontline positions — a war crime, if confirmed. A Ukrainian brigade officer stated that Russian forces are increasing their use of fiber optic cable drones that are resistant to Ukrainian EW, with ranges now exceeding 10 kilometers. Drone operators of the Russian 9th Motorized Rifle Brigade are operating in the direction.[2]

Russian forces recently marginally advanced in Toretsk on January 4. Geolocated footage published on January 4 indicates that Russian forces recently marginally advanced along 1-Hirskyi Street in northwestern Toretsk. Russian forces conducted offensive operations near Toretsk itself, northeast of Toretsk near Dyliivka, and west of Toretsk near Shcherbynivka.[3]
