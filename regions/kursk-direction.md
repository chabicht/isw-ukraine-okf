# Current Situation

Russian forces recently regained positions in the main Ukrainian salient in Kursk Oblast on January 7 amid continued offensive operations. Geolocated footage published on January 6 and 7 indicates Russian forces recently advanced in western Kruglenkoye (northwest of Sudzha), east of Leonidovo (northwest of Sudzha), west of Staraya Sorochina (north of Sudzha), and northwest of Russkoye Porechnoye (north of Sudzha). Russian milbloggers claimed the seizure of Staraya Sorochina, Russkoye Porechnoye, Kositsa, Novosotnitsky, Berdin (all northeast of Sudzha), and Makhnovka (southeast of Sudzha), as well as further advances in multiple areas across the salient, though ISW has not observed confirmation of these claims. A Russian milblogger claimed Russian forces are conducting an unprecedented number of aviation sorties in the Kursk direction.[1]

Ukrainian forces recently advanced in their main salient in Kursk Oblast amid limited offensive operations. Geolocated footage published on January 6 indicates Ukrainian forces recently advanced in northern Pogrebki (northwest of Sudzha). A Russian source claimed on January 7 that Russian forces pushed Ukrainian forces out of Pogrebki. Russian sources claimed that Ukrainian forces unsuccessfully attacked in the Sudzha-Bolshoye Soldatskoye direction, towards Berdin, and near Cherkasskoye Porechnoye. A Russian milblogger claimed that Ukrainian forces hold positions in a contested "gray zone" near Berdin.[1]

Ukraine's Special Operations Forces (SSO) stated on January 7 that Ukrainian forces killed 13 North Korean soldiers in Kursk Oblast.[1]

Elements of the Russian 83rd Airborne (VDV) Brigade, 56th VDV Regiment (7th VDV Division), and "Burevestnik" Detachment (Russian Volunteer Corps) are reportedly operating in Kursk Oblast. Elements of the 34th Motorized Rifle Brigade (49th CAA, SMD) and 155th Naval Infantry Brigade (Pacific Fleet, EMD) are reportedly operating near Malaya Loknya. Elements of the 810th Naval Infantry Brigade (BSF, SMD) are reportedly operating near Bolshoye Soldatskoye. Elements of the 1427th Motorized Rifle Regiment and "Volki" Brigade (Russian Volunteer Corps) are reportedly operating near Russkoye Porechnoye.[1]
