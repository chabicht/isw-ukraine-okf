# Current Situation

Ukrainian forces made tactical advances in at least three areas within the Ukrainian salient in Kursk Oblast on January 6, continuing intensified offensive operations. Geolocated footage published on January 5 and 6 indicates that Ukrainian forces recently advanced in southern Berdin, central Russkoye Porechnoye, and central Novosotnitsky (all northeast of Sudzha). Russian sources reported repelling a roughly reduced platoon-sized Ukrainian mechanized assault near Berdin. Elements of the Russian 30th Motorized Rifle Regiment (72nd Motorized Rifle Division, 44th Army Corps, Leningrad Military District), 2nd Spetsnaz Brigade (GRU), 11th Airborne (VDV) Brigade, and Akhmat Spetsnaz units reportedly cleared areas near Berdin and Novosotnitsky. One Russian milblogger characterized the Ukrainian attacks as enhanced reconnaissance in force operations that could be a diversionary effort for unspecified future operations. ISW assesses these may be the beginning stages of a concerted Ukrainian operation in Kursk Oblast or elsewhere in the theater.[1]

Russian forces attempted to leverage Ukrainian attacks northeast of Sudzha to attack elsewhere in the salient. Geolocated footage indicates Russian forces advanced west of Malaya Loknya (northwest of Sudzha). Russian milbloggers claimed that forces including elements of the 56th VDV Regiment (7th VDV Division) seized Leonidovo (northwest of Sudzha) and that Russian forces advanced in northeastern Russkoye Porechnoye and near Makhnovka and Dmitryukov (southeast of Sudzha), though ISW has not confirmed these claims. Russian forces including elements of the 155th Naval Infantry Brigade (Pacific Fleet, Eastern Military District) attacked toward Malaya Loknya and near Novoivanovka, Viktorovka, and Nikolskiy. The Russian MoD confirmed that "Caspian naval infantry" drone units — likely elements of the 177th Naval Infantry Regiment (Caspian Flotilla) — are operating in Kursk Oblast, indicating the Russian command likely redeployed these elements from western Zaporizhia Oblast.[1]

Ukrainian forces may be continuing long-range strikes against Russian rear areas in Kursk Oblast as part of integrated strike capabilities supporting ground operations. Geolocated footage indicates audible explosions near the Russian 3754th Central Aviation Technical Base in Kursk City. A Russian milblogger claimed Ukrainian forces targeted forward and rear positions with missiles several times. The Russian Kursk Oblast Operational Headquarters claimed Russian forces destroyed two Ukrainian missiles over the oblast.[1]

Ukrainian EW interference during the assaults in Kursk Oblast prevented Russian forces from operating drones in the area, degrading their ability to defend against Ukrainian mechanized attacks. Russian milbloggers noted that drones with fiber optic cables are one of the few variants that consistently resist Ukrainian EW countermeasures.[1]

A Russian source claimed that a Ukrainian missile strike near Lgov, Kursk Oblast killed the Russian 76th VDV Division's Communications Head Lieutenant Colonel Valery Tereschchenko on December 30. A Ukrainian source stated that Ukrainian forces launched Storm Shadow missiles at a 76th VDV Division command post, killing eight senior commanders and wounding 20 personnel.[2]
