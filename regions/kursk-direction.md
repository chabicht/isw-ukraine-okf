# Current Situation

As of January 3, 2025, there were no confirmed frontline changes in Kursk Oblast. A Russian milblogger claimed advances to northeastern Leonidovo and northern Aleksandriya (both southeast of Korenevo), but ISW has not confirmed these claims. Fighting continued southeast of Korenevo near Leonidovo, Aleksandriya, Viktorovka, Nikolaevo-Darino, and Kruglenkoye and south of Sudzha near Kurilovka. One milblogger assessed that Russian forces have not been able to fully seize the initiative in the area.[1]

Drone operators of the Russian 56th VDV Regiment (7th VDV Division), elements of the "Tigr" Volunteer Detachment (BARS), and reconnaissance elements of the "Anvar Spetsnaz" detachment and the "Veterany" Assault Brigade are operating in the direction.[1]

A Russian court ordered Yandex to remove or blur maps and photographs of the Rosneft Ryazan Oil Refinery — struck by Ukrainian drones in March, May, and July 2024 — marking the first judicial move to obfuscate strategically important defense industry facilities after successful Ukrainian strikes.[1]
