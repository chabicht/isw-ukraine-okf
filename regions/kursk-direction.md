# Current Situation

As of January 2, 2025, there were no confirmed frontline changes in Kursk Oblast. Ukrainian forces control roughly 585 sq km of territory within the salient, while Russia has recaptured approximately 483 sq km (roughly 39% of the original Ukrainian salient). North Korean forces continue to suffer casualties in combat operations near Ulanok, Fantasiyevka, and Cherkasskaya Konopelka (all southeast of Sudzha). Elements of the Russian 810th Naval Infantry Brigade (Black Sea Fleet) and the 56th VDV Regiment (7th VDV Division) are operating in the Kursk direction. Ukrainian forces conducted a missile strike, possibly with HIMARS, against the 810th Brigade's command post in Ivanovskoye during an award ceremony, damaging the building and military vehicles. [1]
