# Current Situation

Ukrainian forces resumed offensive operations in at least three areas within the Ukrainian salient in Kursk Oblast and made tactical advances on January 5. Ukrainian forces conducted multiple roughly company-sized mechanized assaults in the Berdin-Novosotnitsky direction (northeast of Sudzha) in three waves of attack using roughly a battalion's worth of armored vehicles. Ukrainian forces also intensified offensive operations in the direction of Leonidovo (southeast of Korenevo) and conducted a reinforced platoon-sized mechanized assault near Pushkarnoye (east of Sudzha). Geolocated footage confirmed Ukrainian forces advanced in fields southwest and south of Berdin and entered the southern part of the settlement. Ukrainian forces also occupy Cherkasskoye Porechnoye, Martynovka, and Mikhaylovka (all northeast of Sudzha) and recently entered Novosotnitsky (just east of Berdin), advancing in fields west of Yamskaya Step and west of Novaya Sorochina. Russian milbloggers expressed concern that the renewed Ukrainian effort may be a diversionary operation.[1]

Russian forces advanced southeast of Sudzha and counterattacked against intensified Ukrainian attacks southeast of Korenevo and north of Sudzha. Geolocated footage confirmed Russian forces advanced in western and southern Makhnovka (just southeast of Sudzha). Russian milbloggers claimed Russian forces also advanced southeast of Makhnovka and along a road into southeastern Kurilovka, and that Russian forces advanced during counterattacks east of Leonidovo towards Nikolskiy and in the direction of Malaya Loknya. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky stated that Russian and North Korean forces lost up to a battalion of infantry near Makhnovka on January 3 and 4.[1]

Elements of the Russian 155th Naval Infantry Brigade (Pacific Fleet, Eastern Military District), Chechen Akhmat "Aida" Spetsnaz group, former Wagner Group personnel, and unspecified BARS units reportedly defended against the Ukrainian effort in Kursk Oblast. Elements of the Russian 106th Airborne (VDV) Division are operating near Sverdlikovo and Lebedevka (both southeast of Korenevo). Elements of the Russian 83rd and 11th VDV brigades continue operating in Kursk Oblast.[1]

A Russian source claimed that a Ukrainian missile strike near Lgov, Kursk Oblast killed the Russian 76th VDV Division's Communications Head Lieutenant Colonel Valery Tereschchenko on December 30. A Ukrainian source stated that Ukrainian forces launched Storm Shadow missiles at a 76th VDV Division command post, killing eight senior commanders and wounding 20 personnel.[2]

Ukrainian EW interference during the assaults in Kursk Oblast prevented Russian forces from operating drones in the area, degrading their ability to defend against Ukrainian mechanized attacks. Russian milbloggers noted that drones with fiber optic cables are one of the few variants that consistently resist Ukrainian EW countermeasures. Russian officials claimed Russian forces downed three unspecified Ukrainian missiles over Kursk Oblast, suggesting Ukrainian forces may be integrating longer-range strike capabilities with ground operations and tactical EW systems. Ukrainian forces conducted HIMARS strikes near Bolshoye Soldatskoye and other areas to prevent Russian forces from deploying reinforcements, artillery systems, and drone operators.[1]
