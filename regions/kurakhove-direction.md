# Current Situation

Russian forces seized Sontsivka (northwest of Kurakhove) and have reportedly captured the Kurakhivske Thermal Power Plant west of Kurakhove, though ISW had not confirmed the latter as of January 2. ISW assesses Russian forces occupy roughly 80% of Kurakhove. A Ukrainian brigade spokesperson reported that Russian forces are using armored vehicles less frequently after significant vehicle losses in October and November 2024 and are bringing in reserves including new recruits with minimal training.[1]

On January 3, Mashovets stated that Ukrainian forces likely completely withdrew from the Kurakhivske TPP and that Russian forces seized Shevchenko (northwest of Kurakhove). A Russian milblogger claimed the seizure of Slovyanka (northwest of Kurakhove), though ISW has not confirmed this. Elements of the Russian 5th and 110th motorized rifle brigades (51st CAA), 6th Tank Regiment, 428th Motorized Rifle Regiment, and 67th Rifle Regiment (all 90th Tank Division) are attacking north of the Vovcha River. Elements of the 20th and 150th motorized rifle divisions (8th CAA) are operating near Maksymilyanivka, Dalne, and Uspenivka.[2]

Ukrainian forces likely maintain limited positions near the Kurakhivske Reservoir as of January 4. Geolocated footage published on January 3 indicates that Ukrainian forces maintain limited positions south of Stari Terny (northwest of Kurakhove) on the western edge of the reservoir. Additional geolocated footage published on January 3 indicates that Russian forces recently marginally advanced northeast of Kostyantynopolske (southwest of Kurakhove). Russian milbloggers claimed that Russian forces are clearing limited Ukrainian positions in western Kurakhove, are trying to bypass Petropavlivka from the north, and advanced along the H-15 highway toward Dachne. Ukrainian forces repelled a platoon-sized mechanized assault in the Kurakhove direction. Drone operators of the Russian 110th Motorized Rifle Brigade (51st CAA) and artillery units of the 238th Artillery Brigade (8th CAA) are operating near the reservoir; elements of the Russian 150th Motorized Rifle Division (8th CAA) are operating near Dachne; elements of the Russian 14th Spetsnaz Brigade (GRU) are operating near Zelenivka; and elements of the 57th Motorized Rifle Brigade (5th CAA, Eastern Military District) are operating in the direction.[3]

Note: ISW is incorporating data previously reported as the Vuhledar direction as part of the Kurakhove direction moving forward, reflecting the shifting operational situation.
