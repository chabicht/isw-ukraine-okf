# Current Situation

Russian forces seized Sontsivka (northwest of Kurakhove) and have reportedly captured the Kurakhivske Thermal Power Plant west of Kurakhove, though ISW has not confirmed the latter. ISW assesses Russian forces occupy roughly 80% of Kurakhove. A Ukrainian brigade spokesperson reported that Russian forces are using armored vehicles less frequently after significant vehicle losses in October and November 2024 and are bringing in reserves including new recruits with minimal training.
