# Current Situation

Russian forces seized Sontsivka (northwest of Kurakhove) and have reportedly captured the Kurakhivske Thermal Power Plant west of Kurakhove, though ISW had not confirmed the latter as of January 2. ISW assesses Russian forces occupy roughly 80% of Kurakhove. A Ukrainian brigade spokesperson reported that Russian forces are using armored vehicles less frequently after significant vehicle losses in October and November 2024 and are bringing in reserves including new recruits with minimal training.[1]

On January 3, Mashovets stated that Ukrainian forces likely completely withdrew from the Kurakhivske TPP and that Russian forces seized Shevchenko (northwest of Kurakhove). A Russian milblogger claimed the seizure of Slovyanka (northwest of Kurakhove), though ISW has not confirmed this. Offensive operations continued near Kurakhove itself, northwest near Slovyanka, Petropavlivka, and Shevchenko, and west near Dachne. Elements of the Russian 5th and 110th motorized rifle brigades (51st CAA), 6th Tank Regiment, 428th Motorized Rifle Regiment, and 67th Rifle Regiment (all 90th Tank Division) are attacking north of the Vovcha River. Elements of the 20th and 150th motorized rifle divisions (8th CAA) are operating near Maksymilyanivka, Dalne, and Uspenivka.[2]
