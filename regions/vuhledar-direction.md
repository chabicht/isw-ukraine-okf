# Current Situation

Russian forces recently advanced north of Kostyantynopolske (northwest of Vuhledar), confirmed by geolocated footage published on December 31. Elements of the Russian 102nd Motorized Rifle Regiment (150th Motorized Rifle Division) are operating near Uspenivka, and the 238th Artillery Brigade is operating near Ulakly. Near Velyka Novosilka, there were no confirmed advances; Ukrainian forces are counterattacking in the area. [1]
