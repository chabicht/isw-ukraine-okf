# Current Situation

Russian forces recently seized Vozdvyzhenka (east of Pokrovsk) and Novovasylivka (southwest of Pokrovsk), and advanced west of Zelene (south of Pokrovsk). Russian forces are advancing towards Udachne (west of Pokrovsk along the T0406 road). The pattern of intensified attacks both southwest of Pokrovsk towards Udachne and east of Pokrovsk near Vozdvyzhenka suggests Russian forces may intend to envelop Pokrovsk from western and eastern flanks rather than frontal assault. Ukrainian officials report Russian forces lack resources for direct frontal assaults on the town's more urbanized areas.[1]

On January 3, geolocated footage confirmed Russian forces recently advanced north of Novohrodivka (southeast of Pokrovsk) and along the T-05-15 Pokrovsk-Novotroitske highway northeast of Shevchenko (south of Pokrovsk). Unconfirmed Russian milblogger claims included advances to Zvirove (southeast of Pokrovsk), northwest of Vozdvyzhenka, one kilometer north of Vovkove, and towards Nadezhdynka. Ukrainian military observer Kostyantyn Mashovets reported that the Russian command is redeploying elements of the 41st CAA from east of Pokrovsk to the area south and southwest of Pokrovsk belonging to the 2nd CAA, shifting focus from enveloping Pokrovsk to pushing west towards the Donetsk-Dnipropetrovsk Oblast border.[2]
