# Current Situation

Russian forces recently seized Vozdvyzhenka (east of Pokrovsk) and Novovasylivka (southwest of Pokrovsk), and advanced west of Zelene (south of Pokrovsk). Russian forces are advancing towards Udachne (west of Pokrovsk along the T0406 road). The pattern of intensified attacks both southwest of Pokrovsk towards Udachne and east of Pokrovsk near Vozdvyzhenka suggests Russian forces may intend to envelop Pokrovsk from western and eastern flanks rather than frontal assault. Ukrainian officials report Russian forces lack resources for direct frontal assaults on the town's more urbanized areas.[1]

On January 3, geolocated footage confirmed Russian forces recently advanced north of Novohrodivka (southeast of Pokrovsk) and along the T-05-15 Pokrovsk-Novotroitske highway northeast of Shevchenko (south of Pokrovsk). Ukrainian military observer Kostyantyn Mashovets reported that the Russian command is redeploying elements of the 41st CAA from east of Pokrovsk to the area south and southwest of Pokrovsk belonging to the 2nd CAA, shifting focus from enveloping Pokrovsk to pushing west towards the Donetsk-Dnipropetrovsk Oblast border.[2]

Russian forces recently advanced southeast and southwest of Pokrovsk on January 4. Geolocated footage published on January 4 indicates that Russian forces advanced in central Lysivka (southeast of Pokrovsk) and northwest of Vovkove (southwest of Pokrovsk), indicating that Russian forces have likely seized Vovkove. Russian milbloggers claimed further advances northeast of Pokrovsk from Vozdvyzhenka and north of Tymofiivka, and that Russian forces are one kilometer from the T-05-04 Pokrovsk-Kostyantynivka highway. Ukraine's Khortytsia Group of Forces Spokesperson Major Viktor Trehubov stated that Russian forces are attempting to envelop Pokrovsk to avoid urban combat. Russian sources credited elements of the 30th Motorized Rifle Brigade (2nd CAA, Central Military District) and 80th Tank Regiment (90th Tank Division, 41st CAA, CMD) with the seizure of Novoolenivka (southwest of Pokrovsk).[3]
