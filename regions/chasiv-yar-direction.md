# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations in the Chasiv Yar direction but made no confirmed advances on January 1 and 2. Russian milbloggers claimed advances in northern Chasiv Yar and near the Refractory Plant, with one claiming Russian forces control most of the Pivnichnyi Microraion. Russian forces are conducting heavy glide bomb and drone strikes on the Refractory Plant to prepare for future ground assaults. Ukrainian officials report Russian forces are mostly conducting infantry assaults with limited mechanized assaults, using fog and smoke grenades for concealment. Elements of the Russian 98th VDV Division are operating in the area. [1]
