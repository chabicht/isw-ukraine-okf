# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations in the Chasiv Yar direction on January 3 but made no confirmed advances. Russian forces attacked within and near Chasiv Yar itself and south of Chasiv Yar near Bila Hora, Predtechyne, and Stupochky. A Russian milblogger claimed Ukrainian forces are counterattacking near the Refractory Plant in central Chasiv Yar. Drone operators of the Russian 7th Military Base (49th CAA) are operating in the area.[1]
