# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations in the Chasiv Yar direction on January 7 but did not make any confirmed advances. Russian forces conducted offensive operations within Chasiv Yar itself, northeast of Chasiv Yar near Bondarne and Vasyukivka, and south of Chasiv Yar near Bila Hora and Stupochky. Drone operators of the Russian 98th Airborne (VDV) Division reportedly continue to operate near and within Chasiv Yar.[1]
