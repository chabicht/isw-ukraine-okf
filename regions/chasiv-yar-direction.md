# Current Situation

Russian forces continued offensive operations in the Chasiv Yar direction on January 4 but did not make any confirmed advances. Russian forces conducted offensive operations within and near Chasiv Yar itself, southeast of Chasiv Yar near Klishchiivka, and south of Chasiv Yar near Bila Hora and towards Predtechyne and Stupochky. The commander of a Ukrainian brigade operating in the direction stated that the Russian military command has committed over 10,000 personnel to offensive operations near Chasiv Yar. Elements of the Russian 98th Airborne (VDV) Division and 7th Military Base (49th Combined Arms Army, Southern Military District) are reportedly operating near Chasiv Yar.[1]
