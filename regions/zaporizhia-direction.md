# Current Situation

Russian forces recently advanced in northern Bilohirya (northeast of Robotyne), confirmed by geolocated footage published on January 2. Elements of the Russian 70th Motorized Rifle Regiment (42nd Motorized Rifle Division) conducted a raid into central Bilohirya before withdrawing. Bilohirya remains a contested gray zone. Fighting continued near Novoandriivka (northwest of Robotyne). Elements of the 38th Motorized Rifle Brigade (35th CAA) are operating in the Polohy direction.[1]

Russian forces continued offensive operations in western Zaporizhia Oblast on January 3 but made no confirmed advances. Drone operators of the Russian 247th VDV Regiment (7th VDV Division) are operating in the Orikhiv direction (north of Robotyne).[2]

Russian forces continued offensive operations in western Zaporizhia Oblast on January 4 but did not make any confirmed advances. A Russian milblogger claimed advances northwest of Robotyne near Mala Tokmachka and Bilohirya, but ISW has not observed confirmation. A Russian milblogger claimed that Ukrainian forces counterattacked in unspecified areas in western Zaporizhia Oblast. Drone operators of the Russian BARS-1 Detachment and 247th VDV Regiment continue operating in the Orikhiv direction.[3]

Russian forces continued offensive operations in western Zaporizhia Oblast near Novodanylivka (north of Robotyne) on January 7 but did not make any confirmed advances. A Russian milblogger claimed Ukrainian forces are attempting to advance in small infantry groups toward Vasylivka (west of Robotyne and southeast of Kamyanske). Elements of the Russian 291st Motorized Rifle Regiment (42nd Motorized Rifle Division, 58th CAA, SMD) and BARS-Sarmat Detachment (Russian Combat Reserve) are reportedly operating in the Zaporizhia direction.[4]

Russian forces continued offensive operations in western Zaporizhia Oblast on January 8 but did not make any confirmed advances. The spokesperson of a Ukrainian brigade operating in the Orikhiv direction reported on January 8 that Russian forces are conducting small infantry assaults several times per day in an attempt to identify weaknesses in Ukrainian defensive lines, and are largely not using armored vehicles in assaults in this area. The Russian Ministry of Defense stated that elements of the Russian BARS-11 "Kuban" Detachment (Russian Combat Army Reserve) are training in a rear area in the Zaporizhia direction to prepare for future combat operations near Orikhiv. Elements of the Russian BARS-Sarmat Detachment and the 7th Airborne (VDV) Division are reportedly operating in the Kamyanske direction (northwest of Robotyne).[5]
